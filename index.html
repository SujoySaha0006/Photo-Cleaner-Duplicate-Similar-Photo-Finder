Safety Guarantees

  • At least one photograph is always preserved in every duplicate or similar group.
  • Protected photographs can never be marked by Smart Auto Mark or manual selection.
  • Browsers cannot silently delete files from your Windows drive — cleanup generates a verified Windows PowerShell script that moves only your confirmed files to the Windows Recycle Bin.

Confirm Safe Cleanup

Every group keeps at least one copy, and protected photos are excluded. Confirming removes these copies from active groups (with 1-click Undo in History) and lets you download a 1-click Windows Recycle Bin script.
